nondual poster

A small personal automation that publishes one quiet, non-dual reflection each day — an image or a short ambient video with a single thought — to the connected social media accounts of its owner (Instagram and TikTok: @nondual.normal).

The app posts only to accounts owned and authorized by the operator. It serves no ads, tracks no visitors, and collects no data from anyone else.
