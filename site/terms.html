1. What this app is

"nondual poster" is a personal, non-commercial automation tool. It publishes one daily post (an image or short video with a contemplative quote) to social media accounts that belong to and are explicitly authorized by the app's operator.

2. Who may use it

The app is operated privately for the operator's own accounts. It is not offered as a service to third parties, and no third-party accounts can connect to it.

3. Content

All published content is created by the operator (original graphics, own photographs, and royalty-free licensed music). Content is intended to be calm, non-commercial, and suitable for a general audience.

4. Acceptable use

The app complies with the platform policies of the services it posts to, including the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. It posts at most a small, fixed number of times per day and does not send spam, engage with other users, or collect data from anyone.
